The scope: Our project is a movement style first person shooter.Based on Doom and other similar shooters.The game is hell themed similar to doom, but the main player is a demon himself. Our character has fire based powers, and is able to wield a plethora of weapons.The map starts off as an office building, our MC finds out that he is getting fired soon. He moves through the office to get to his boss (potential boss fight or just cutscene) and starts a fight. The boss explains that he is not in control of the situation and our character sets off to go deeper into hell to get to the big boss and get his job back. The city is based on wall street. Baal street

Deliverables: What needs to be done is creating a map, gun module, enemy modules, movement for both the player and enemies, moving between maps, levels, achievements and the visual look. Checkpoint 1: The map The map is based on hell. We will have to find textures online for our map. We will create the borders, determine its size, colors and theme. We will then learn how to do loading screens and moving between levels.

Checkpoint 2: The player(Movement and camera) This is where we will make the player. We need to work on movement. Not just WASD, but speed, jumping, sliding and movement mechanics.

Checkpoint 3: Weapons(The model, types and firing) We will make a simple model of a gun, code the firing of the gun, speed of the projectile launched, reloading and reloading animation and then extend this to other weapons. We will make a swinging animation for melee weapons. The length of a reload, the reach of a weapon, the bullet speed, the size of the bullet and hitboxes.

Checkpoint 4 : Enemies: Enemy hitboxes. Bosses. Attack patterns. Enemy gimmicks (Example: Some are melee based, some are ranged, some might charge and some might jump.) Enemy designs. 2.5D for the enemies and its pixel art.

Checkpoint 5: Items (Model and game integration) The point where we start making in-game items to diversify the playstile of the game. Featuring cursed objects that alter movement speed, fire rate, jumping, visual cues, and even add more complex functions like gaining additional bullets, life steal, e.t.c.

Checkpoint 6: Menus and GUI’s We will need to create simple working menus like a pause menu, main screen, settings, and maybe options. The GUI that is displayed while playing will also need to be made. This will include a hotbar to show what weapons you currently have, abilities, and important info like health and shield status. Things like the level name, and time can also be included. Pop-up tutorials are also included here.

Checkpoint 7: Sound and effects We will create sound and effects here. The sound of footsteps, ambient sounds, gun firing and enemy sounds. The sound of a gun either hitting a wall or an enemy will be different. The body vs the head will make different sounds when hit. Headshots are critical hits and make distinct sounds.
